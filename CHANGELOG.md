## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- Upload photos from local folders to Google Photos albums on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- Folder structure is automatically mapped to Google Photos albums using the folder name as the album name.
- Select which folders and photos to include before uploading.
- Rename albums and photos before uploading.
- Pre-upload summary showing the number of albums to create and photos to upload.
- Post-upload report showing upload results per album, including skipped and failed photos.
- Cancel an ongoing upload at any time.
- Google account sign-in and sign-out.
- Language preference: English, Dutch, or system default.
- Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Licenses and Third-party software attributions (NOTICES) are accessible in the app.
